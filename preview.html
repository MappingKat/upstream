📖 Key SOPs

SOP-001: Daily Plant Walkthrough
Daily
  1. Check and record chlorine residual at entry point and distribution (target: 1.5–3.0 mg/L free chlorine)
  2. Verify turbidity reading on combined filter effluent (must be ≤0.3 NTU; goal ≤0.1 NTU)
  3. Inspect chemical feed systems — check chlorine tank level, coagulant feed rate, fluoride pump operation
  4. Walk pump stations — listen for unusual sounds, check pressure gauges, note VFD readings
  5. Verify SCADA data matches field readings (>15% deviation = recalibrate sensor)
  6. Check tank levels against expected fill/draw-down curves for time of day
  7. Log all readings in daily operations log and SCADA
Based on AWWA Water Treatment Operator Handbook · Last reviewed: Jan 2026 · Next review: Jul 2026
SOP-004: Chlorine Residual Low Response
Response
  1. Confirm reading with handheld DPD test (HACH Pocket Colorimeter or equivalent)
  2. If confirmed <1.0 mg/L at entry point: increase chlorine feed rate by 0.5 mg/L increments
  3. Check chlorine tank level and injection point for air locks or clogged check valves
  4. If distribution residual <0.2 mg/L: notify supervisor immediately — this is below CDPHE minimum
  5. Sample for total coliform at low-residual points within 4 hours
  6. Document all actions and readings every 15 minutes until residual recovers above 1.5 mg/L
  7. If residual unrecoverable in 2 hours, evaluate need for Tier 1 public notification per Colorado Reg 11
Ref: Colorado Reg 11 §11.8, AWWA M20 (Chlorination Practices) · Last reviewed: Feb 2026
SOP-007: Pump Bearing Inspection & Replacement
Maintenance
  1. Lockout/tagout per OSHA 1910.147 — verify zero energy state before any work
  2. Record current vibration reading (baseline: <0.15 in/s; alarm: >0.3 in/s per ISO 10816)
  3. Check bearing temperature with IR thermometer (normal: 140-160°F; alarm: >180°F)
  4. If replacement needed: order matching bearing (SKF 6310-2RS or equivalent per pump spec)
  5. Drain pump, disconnect coupling, pull impeller assembly
  6. Press out old bearing, clean housing, press in new bearing (use bearing heater for interference fit)
  7. Reassemble, verify alignment (<0.002" TIR), refill and test run for 30 min monitoring temp and vibration
  8. Log in asset management system with new bearing date and runtime hours
Ref: Hydraulic Institute Standards, SKF Bearing Maintenance Guide · Last reviewed: Dec 2025
