🎯 The Competitive White Space

The market gap is clear: Big platforms (Xylem, Veolia, Autodesk) target utilities with 50+ staff and $100K+ budgets. Single-use tools (Fracta, VODA) solve one problem (pipes) but don't address daily operations. Analytics platforms (Power BI, Seeq) require data science skills rural utilities don't have. Upstream AI is the only platform built for the 3-person rural utility: predictive maintenance + compliance + grant guidance in a single affordable package ($12K/year) that works in 30 days. The 45,000 small U.S. water systems have been completely ignored — until now.
