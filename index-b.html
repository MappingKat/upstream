The Problem

Alarm fatigue is a crisis,
not an inconvenience.

A typical small utility generates hundreds of alarms per week. Operators learn to ignore most of them — because the data isn't trustworthy. When you can't trust your sensors, you can't trust your AI. And when something actually fails, you're already numb to the noise.

We don't start with predictions. We start with data quality. Because intelligence built on bad data is worse than no intelligence at all.
