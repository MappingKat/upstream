Target Criteria & Notes

Priority ranking methodology:

  • High Priority: 1–4 staff, <2,000 connections, recent USDA grant activity or compliance pressure, aging infrastructure (>40 years), minimal SCADA automation. USDA grant recipients are priority #1.
  • Medium Priority: 2–8 staff, 2,000–6,500 connections, functional SCADA but likely no predictive capability, known seasonal demand variability.
  • Low Priority: Larger districts (>4,500 connections), more resourced, may already have vendor relationships or in-house IT.

USDA grant recipients (TOP 3 TARGETS):

  • Round Mountain ($4.9M): Brand new mechanical wastewater system. Perfect pilot — desperately need monitoring for new infrastructure.
  • Romeo ($559K): Tiny town (250 connections), new constant pressure pumps + remote meters. Ideal small utility profile.
  • Center ($11.8M): Massive investment to replace non-compliant lagoons. Brand new mechanical treatment = high predictive maintenance need.

Contact webpage column: Direct links to staff/contact pages where available. For facilities without public staff directories, general town/district website provided with "(estimate)" note — you'll need to call main line and ask for operator/manager.

Next steps: Start with USDA grant recipients (Round Mountain, Romeo, Center). Then target other "High Priority" facilities with 1-4 staff. Lead with grant guidance + compliance automation + predictive maintenance bundle.
