Regulatory Compliance

PFAS regulations are here.
Is your utility ready?

EPA's first-ever PFAS drinking water rule sets maximum contaminant levels at 4 ppt for PFOA and PFOS. The clock is already ticking for small utilities.

Deadline: April 2027

Initial monitoring required

All public water systems must complete initial PFAS sampling and report results. Upstream AI automates your sampling calendar, tracks lab submissions, and flags upcoming deadlines before they're missed.

Deadline: April 2029

MCL compliance required

PFOA and PFOS must be below 4 parts per trillion. Systems exceeding limits must notify the public and install treatment. Early monitoring data now means better capital planning before the deadline hits.

Ongoing

Annual monitoring & reporting

Continuous reporting obligations after initial monitoring. Upstream AI generates submission-ready compliance reports, tracks results against MCL thresholds, and alerts operators when values trend toward limits.
