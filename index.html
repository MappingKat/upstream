Solutions

The questions operators
ask every single day.

Can I trust this reading? Who changed that setpoint? Did I miss something on the night shift? We answer these questions first — then add intelligence on top.

Data Trust

"Is this sensor reading real?"

Every value gets a trust score. "Chlorine: 2.3 ppm (Trust: 94%)" means act on it. "Chlorine: 2.3 ppm (Trust: 38%, probe overdue calibration)" means verify manually. No more 3 AM drives to check a reading that turns out to be a drifting probe.

0-100% trust score on every data point
Daily Observations

"I'm still logging everything on paper."

Replace paper logs and spreadsheets with a cloud-based process journal. Enter manual readings, lab results, and field notes once — they sync to compliance records automatically. Guardrails flag out-of-range values the moment you type them in.

Paper → cloud guardrail alerts on every entry
Visual Monitoring

"I can't check the basins every hour."

Connect cameras to your basins and pump room. The AI runs a visual inspection every hour — detecting foam buildup, equipment vibration, and surface anomalies. Your physical visits focus on what the system has already flagged, not routine look-arounds.

Hourly AI visual checks replace manual site visits
Intelligent Alerts

"Pump 2 bearing will fail in 4-7 days"

Once you trust the data, predictions become valuable. But we show our work: "87% confident. Based on: vibration trend +15% over 5 days, temperature +8°F, runtime 2,847 hrs." You understand the why — and you decide whether to act now or monitor another day.

48-72hr advance warning before failures
